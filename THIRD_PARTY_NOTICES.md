# Third-Party Notices

Spectra is licensed under the MIT License (see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)). It
includes, fetches at build time, or redistributes the third-party components
listed below. Each component remains under its own license; full license texts
are in [`licenses/`](licenses/). Installs and CPack packages place this file,
`LICENSE` and `licenses/` under `share/doc/spectra/`.

How a component reaches you:

- **vendored**: the source is committed under `third_party/`.
- **fetched**: CMake `FetchContent` downloads it at configure time, pinned to
  the tag listed, and it is compiled into Spectra's libraries/executables.
  Where a `SPECTRA_USE_SYSTEM_<DEP>` option exists, a system copy can be used
  instead (see `BUILD_ENVIRONMENT.md`). In that case the system package's own
  license applies and Spectra does not redistribute it.
- **embedded asset**: font data is compiled into the binaries and/or installed
  under `share/spectra/fonts/`.
